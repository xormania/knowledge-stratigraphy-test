# Governance

Knowledge Stratigraphy Test is an MIT-licensed personal project maintained by
**xor (`@xormania`)**. Outside contributions are welcome; the owner retains final scope,
release, moderation, and merge authority. A template, older design note, or automated
check does not overrule a later explicit owner decision.

There is no steering committee, artificial reviewer quorum, CLA, or requirement for a
public legal name. Normal outside PRs need useful evidence, green checks, and owner
review. CODEOWNERS routes reviews to `@xormania`; owner-administrator bypass remains
available for maintenance without requiring someone to approve their own work.

New maintainers or automation with write access are explicit owner decisions. Bots do
not self-promote, approve their own PRs, or merge based solely on a filled template.
AI-assisted submissions are judged by the same evidence and maintenance standards as
other submissions. Contribution quality, not PR volume, is the goal.

Use [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for acceptance requirements and
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for community expectations. Disagreements should
identify the evidence and tradeoff; the owner can decline a technically valid change
that does not serve the project.
